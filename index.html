Questions, answered

Is Toma free?

Yes. Toma is free to use.

Do I need an account?

No. You can try everything as a guest on this device. Create an account to keep your habits, moods and progress across devices.

Is my data private?

When you sign in, your data is protected by row-level security, so only your account can read or change it.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Toma is built mobile-first and adapts from phones to big screens.

Can I make my own habits and timers?

Absolutely. Add, edit or delete habits, and create focus timers with any name and length.
